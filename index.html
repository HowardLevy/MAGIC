Sharing at MAGiC

For members and invited outside speakers

Sharing your expertise is a gift to the community.

We are a community of similarly gifted and experienced professionals. We believe that we get by giving — it is our focus and our primary activity. Many members report that they have benefited from referrals or resources from others.

MAGiC is not a "networking" group. As a result, making a pitch is counterproductive and will diminish your own MAGiC. We think of you more highly than that.

Request a speaking date
Considerations
Not a pitch
The group is a community, not a sales opportunity.
Seasoned audience
Members are experienced marketers seeking advanced insights.
A conversation
Sessions are interactive, with 20+ members attending each week.
Transparency
Share behind-the-scenes details and lessons learned.
Before you share
  1. 01 Arrange a date with Leslie Lawton, who graciously coordinates our calendar.
  2. 02 Three weeks before your date, send Leslie and Howard Levy — who publishes our weekly newsletter — a brief description of what you will share and what the audience will gain, a one or two sentence bio, a headshot or other image, and links to your LinkedIn, webpage or book.
  3. 03 On the day you present, sign on ten minutes early so our tech is in sync. Let us know whether you prefer questions at the end or throughout.
  4. 04 Consider joining a week or two beforehand to get a feel for how we engage. Most of all, come to enjoy meeting people who are looking forward to hearing from you — and think about how we can help you.

Need a recommendation, an introduction, a vendor, a second opinion? Send your question to the Exchange and it goes out to the whole community by email. Answers come back from people who have actually done it.
